<p>
I am a Post-doctoral researcher at in the Faculty of Science and Engineering at Southern Cross University. My research focuses on iron mineral formation and transformation in soils and aquatic environments. As a biogeochemist and environmental mineralogist, I investigate the influence of mineral transformation on the availability and mobility of pollutants and nutrients in the environment. I am interested in the effects of complex environmental matrices on mineral processes.
</p>
<p>
Key Research Interests:
  <ul>
  <li>Acid mine drainage and acid sulfate soils.</li>
    <li>Environmental mineralogy and trace element behaviour.</li>
    <li>Iron oxhyhydroxide and hydroxysulfates including jarosite, schwertmannite and ferrihydrite.</li>
    <li>Iron and aluminium geochemistry.</li>
    <li>Mössbauer, Synchrotron XAS and Raman spectroscopy.</li>
    <li>Effects of nano-scale heterogeneity in environmental mineralogy.</li>
  </ul>
  </p>
